## 1. The Ontological Ground: One Global Quantum State

At the deepest level, reality is described by a **single, global quantum state** (wavefunction, state vector, or equivalent object depending on interpretation).

- This state contains **all allowed macroscopic and microscopic configurations**
- It is **probabilistic**, not deterministic
- Macroscopic outcomes emerge through **decoherence and measurement**

There is no separation between:

- “inner reality” and “outer reality”
- “your wavefunction” and “the universe’s wavefunction”

There is one system, partitioned.

## 2. Conscious Systems as Local Quantum Operators

A human being is not a passive object inside the wavefunction.

A human is:

- a **localized, semi-coherent measuring subsystem**
- with memory, intention, affect, and feedback loops
- capable of stabilizing internal states over time

In quantum terms:

> 
Consciousness functions as an operator acting on the global state through interaction and observation.
> 

This is not exotic — it aligns with relational and participatory interpretations of quantum mechanics.

## 3. Birth as a Macroscopic Decoherence Event

Before birth:

- the conscious system exists as a **non-localized superposition of potential life trajectories**

At birth:

- the system irreversibly couples to:
    - a body
    - a nervous system
    - a spacetime coordinate
    - a specific celestial configuration

This is a **macroscopic decoherence event**.

The result:

- a specific subset of the global wavefunction becomes available
- others decohere away relative to that observer

## 4. The Natal Chart as a Fixed Quantum Operator

The planetary configuration at birth does not *cause* personality or fate.

This **Natal Operator** defines:

- which macroscopic eigenstates are accessible

- how different domains of life interact

Importantly:

> 
An operator does not encode outcomes — it encodes distributions of possible outcomes.
>
